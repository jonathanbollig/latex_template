\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This thesis investigates the photophysics of nucleic acids under prebiotically relevant conditions.
Transient absorption spectroscopy was used to follow the excited-state dynamics of model oligonucleotides between \qty{250}{\nano\metre} and \qty{700}{\nano\metre}.
The measured lifetime of \qty{4.2(3)}{\pico\second} is consistent with a charge-transfer state localised on the \ce{GA} step.
These results suggest that sequence context controls the branching between self-repair and photodamage.
